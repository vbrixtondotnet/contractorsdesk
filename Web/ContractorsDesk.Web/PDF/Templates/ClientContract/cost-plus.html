Article 5: Progress Payments

5.1. Progress Payments. Owner shall make or cause to be made progress payments within ten (10) days to Contractor for all amounts submitted in approved Construction Draw Requests. These requests shall be based upon work performed. Any delays caused by the lack of response from the Owner or their agents shall result in a comparable period being added to the completion date of construction.

5.2. Time for Making Progress Payments. Progress payments shall be paid by the Owner or Lending Institution within ten (10) days of the Contractor's submission of each Construction Draw Request, except that payment may be withheld if:

  • (a) Contractor's work is defective and has not been remedied.
  • (b) Contractor has not made prompt and proper payment to subcontractors.
  • (c) Claims or liens have been filed against the project.

5.3. Increased Costs Due to Change Orders. Additional costs arising from change orders or material/labor price increases shall be borne by the Owner. The Contractor will make best efforts to adhere to the budget.

5.4. Final Payment. Final payment shall be made to the Contractor within thirty (30) days after completion of the work, provided that:

  • (a) A complete release of all liens is delivered, or a bond indemnifying the Owner is provided.
  • (b) The work is approved by the Owner.

If any disputes arise regarding disapproved work, arbitration will be pursued to resolve the matter.

5.5. Waiver of Claims. The Owner waives all claims upon making the final payment, except for latent defects and those documented in writing before final payment.

5.6. Caveat. Payments withheld due to disputes shall be limited to the value of defective work, claims, or unpaid subcontractor costs. If the Contractor provides a sufficient bond, withheld payments shall be released promptly.

5.7. Occupancy. The Owner shall not occupy the residence until final inspections and payments are completed.

Article 6: Duties

6.1. Duties of the Contractor

6.1.1. Ensure all construction complies with plans and specifications.
6.1.2. Perform all construction in a workmanlike manner compliant with applicable codes and laws.
6.1.3. Utilize licensed individuals for all applicable work.
6.1.4. Obtain necessary permits for the work.
6.1.5. Provide finish materials as per contract, utilizing contractor's discounts. Owner-provided items require contractor approval.

6.2. Duties of the Owner

6.2.1. Provide a full specification book by dates specified in the attached materials form.
6.2.2. Supply interior elevations and styles for bathrooms, kitchens, and cabinetry. Delays in providing these documents may result in additional costs.
6.2.3. Assume responsibility for owner-purchased items installed by the contractor.

Article 7: Change Orders and Finish Schedules

7.1. A change order must be agreed upon in writing (email or text), including cost, time considerations, and work details.
7.2. Delays or changes in finish selection schedules may extend the completion date.

Article 8: Insurance

8.1. The Owner shall maintain a property insurance policy until construction is completed.
8.2. Property insurance must cover the full insurable value against fire, vandalism, and other risks.
8.3. The Contractor will maintain required Workman's Compensation and Liability insurance.

Article 9: General Provisions

9.1. Unforeseen site conditions causing cost or schedule adjustments will be addressed through mutual agreement, with costs borne by the Owner.

Article 10: Hazardous Materials

10.1. Discovery of hazardous materials will require specialized contractors. Associated costs and time adjustments will be treated as a change order.

Article 11: Arbitration of Disputes

11.1. Any disputes will be settled through arbitration under the American Arbitration Association's Construction Industry Arbitration Rules.

Article 12: Warranty

12.1. The warranty for workmanship or materials is managed directly between the Owner and subcontractors/suppliers. Legal actions must be initiated within one year of project completion.

Article 13: Termination of Contract

13.1. In case of default by either party, damages, specific performance, or liquidated damages may be pursued as stipulated.

Article 14: Attorney Fees

14.1. The prevailing party in any dispute shall be entitled to reasonable attorney fees and costs.

Article 15: Acceptance and Occupancy

15.1. Upon completion, inspections shall occur, and necessary repairs shall be made.
15.2. The Owner may not occupy the property until final payment and a Certificate of Occupancy are secured.
15.3. Occupancy in violation of the agreement waives any claims for defects or uncompleted work.
