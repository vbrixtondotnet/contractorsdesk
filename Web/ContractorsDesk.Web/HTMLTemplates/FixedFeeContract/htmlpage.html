Article 4. CHANGES

 

4.1 Nature of Change. If Owner or Contractor requests, or one of the parties believes that a change in the Work is necessary (a “Change”), then the parties shall comply with the following procedures to reflect a Change in the Work:

a. The party requesting or noting the Change shall write a description of the Change and give the other party that writing (the “Change Notice”);

 

b. Before proceeding with the changed Work, unless excused by an emergency involving safety or property damage, Contractor shall provide Owner with a fixed-price written estimate of the cost and time impact of the requested Change.

 

c. If and to the extent that the requested Change relates to the site work and vertical construction of the Project in compliance with the Plans, then no additional supervision fee shall be paid or payable to Contractor in connection with such Change, and Contractor agrees that Contractor’s Supervision Fee shall compensate Contractor for any such Change. However, if and to the extent that the requested Change relates to any work outside the scope of the site work and vertical construction of the Project in compliance with the Plans (e.g., off-site improvements, landscaping, the construction or installation of a pool or sauna, then Owner shall pay Contractor a supervision fee in an amount equal to ten_ percent(10%) of the Cost of Work relating to such Change (any such fee, a “Contractor’s Additional Supervision Fee”).

 

d. Owner and Contractor shall execute a change order (each, a “Change Order”) confirming their agreement with the Change, the fixed-price costand the extension of anticipated completion date, if any. If the Change cannot be performed on a fixed-price basis, the Change Order shall identify the agreed methodof compensation.

 

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary set forth herein, Contractor shall be compensated for Changes necessitated by the enactment or revisions of applicable codes, laws or regulations following the EffectiveDate.Contractor’s right to receive a Contractor’s Additional Supervision Fee in connection with any such Changes shall be determined in accordance with Section 4.1c. above.

 

4.2 Fifty percent(50%) of the Cost of Work of each Change Order (together with Contractor’s Additional Supervision Fee related thereto (if applicable) shall be paid prior to the Change, with the final fifty percent (50%) of the Cost of Work of each Change Order (together with Contractor’s Additional Supervision Fee related thereto (if applicable) paid upon completion of the Change Order.

 

4.3 Change Order Format.A blank Change Order form is included in this Contract as Exhibit C.

17.1.2 Certificates of insurance shall be delivered to Owner prior to commencement of the Work. The Certificate of Insurance shall provide that there will be no cancellation of coverage without at least thirty (30) days’ prior written notice to Owner. If any reduction of coverage occurs, Contractor shall promptly furnish Owner with information regarding such reduction.

17.1.3.1 Worker’s Compensation and Employer’s Liability Insurance; for Contractor and all Sub Contractors performing Work

 

17.1.3.2 Liability Insurance: Commercial General Liability policy form CG 00 01 providing insurance for Bodily Injury and Property Damage Liability and including coverage for:

● Personal Injury Liability, and

● Contractual Liability insuring the tort liability obligations assumed by Contractor in this Contract

 

17.1.3.3 Automobile Bodily Injury and Property Damage Liability Insurance including coverage for all owned, hired and non-owned automobiles.

 

17.1.4 Prior to performing any Work, each Subcontractor and Sub-subcontractor shall procure and maintain insurance asset forth in Section 17.1.3 above.

 

17.2 Owner’s Liability Insurance

 

17.2.1 Owner shall secure and maintain liability insurance to protect Owner. Owner, at its option, may purchase and maintain additional liability insurance to protect Owner against claims arising out of performance of the Work under this Contract. Contractor shall not be responsible for purchasing liability insurance for Owner.

 

17.3 Property Insurance

 

17.3.1 Owner shall, prior to commencement of the Work, secure and maintain property insurance (in the form of Builders Risk Insurance or other suitable cover) in the amount of the original Contract Price from an insurer authorized to do business in the State of California. The amount of insurance shall be adjusted, from time to time, to reflect modifications to the Contract Price. The property insurance shall be maintained until the Work has been completed and accepted by Owner and Final Payment has been made as provided in this Contract. Property insurance shall cover Owner, Contractor, Subcontractors and Sub-subcontractors for their interest in the Work.

 

17.3.1.1 Property Insurance shall be provided on an all-risk policy form and shall insure against the perils of fire and extended coverage and for other physical loss or damage, including coverage for theft, vandalism, malicious mischief, collapse, debris removal (including demolition occasioned by enforcement of any applicable legal requirements) and shall also cover reasonable compensation for Architect/Engineer’s services and expenses required because of such insured loss.The insurance will also cover offsite and transit exposures. Insurance for loss caused by flood, surface waters and earthquake shall not be required unless otherwise provided in this Contract, and Owner shall bear the responsibility for such loss.

 

17.3.1.2 Prior to the first date on which any risk of an exposure to loss may occur, Owner shall provide Contractor with a complete copy of each policy that includes insurance coverages required by this Section 17.3.The policy shall contain a provision that it will not be canceled unless at least thirty (30) days’ prior written notice has been given to Contractor.

 

17.3.2 At Owner’s option, Owner may secure insurance to insure Owner against loss of use of Owner’s property due to fire or other hazards, however caused. Owner waives all rights of action against Contractor, Subcontractors, Sub-subcontractors and their respective employees, agents and representatives for loss of use of Owner’s property, including consequential losses due to fire or other hazards, however caused.

 

17.3.3 If Contractor makes a timely written request that insurance for risks and perils other than those required herein be included in the property insurance policy, Owner shall use its best efforts to secure pricing for such insurance coverage. If the price is acceptable to Contractor, the cost of such added coverage shall be charged to Contractor by appropriate Change Order.

 

17.3.4 If Owner insures other real or personal properties near the Property, by property insurance under policies separate from those insuring the Project, or if after final payment, property insurance is provided on the completed Project through a policy or policies other than those insuring the Project during the construction period, Owner shall waive all rights in accordance with the terms of Section 17.3.6 for damages caused by perils covered by this separate property insurance. All separate policies shall provide this waiver of subrogation by endorsement.

 

17.3.5 Owner and Contractor waive all rights against each other and against all other Subcontractors and Sub-subcontractors for loss or damage to the extent reimbursed by any property or equipment insurance applicable to the Work, except such rights as they may have to the proceeds of such insurance. If any applicable policies of insurance require an endorsement or consent of the insurance company to provide for continued coverage where there is a waiver of subrogation, the owners of such policies will cause them to be so endorsed or obtain such consent

 

17.3.6 Owner shall have no right to partial occupancy or use of the Project and the same shall not commence until the insurance company providing property insurance has consented to such partial occupancy or use of the Project. Owner and Contractor shall take reasonable steps to obtain consent of the insurance company and shall not take action with respect to partial occupancy or use of the Project that would cause cancellation, lapse or reduction of the required property insurance.
