Article 5.               Progress Payments

5.1       Progress Payments. Owner shall make or cause to be made progress payments within ten (10) days to Contractor of all amounts submitted in approved Construction Draw Requests submitted by Contractor to Owner, his designated representative, escrow agent, or Lending Institution, as the work progresses. The contractor shall be entitled to receive Construction Draws in the amount set forth in the Construction Draw Requests, based upon the work performed. Any delays caused by the lack of response by the Owner or his agents shall result in a comparable period of time being added to the completion date of construction. 

5.2       Time for Making Progress Payments. Progress payments shall be paid by 
Owner or the Lending Institution within ten (10) days of the Contractor’s submission to 
Owner of each Construction Draw Request, except that the Owner or Lending Institution may withhold progress payments if written notice is given to Contractor within such ten(10) day period that (a) Contractor’s work is defective and any such defect has not been remedied or cured by Contractor, (b) Contractor has not made prompt and proper payment to subcontractors for labor or materials, or (c) claims or liens have been filed against the Project.

5.3       Increase Costs Due to Change Orders or material/labor price increases are to be born by the owner. Contractor will use their best efforts to stay as close to the budget as possible. 

5.4       Final Payment. Owner shall make final payment to Contractor of all sums due under this Agreement, including the Contractor’s fee, within thirty (30) days after Contractor gives notice to Owner that the Work is completed, subject to the condition that final payment shall not be due until Contractor has delivered to Owner or its agents a complete release of all liens arising out of the Project or in the alternative, a bond sufficient to indemnify Owner against such liens or potential liens. It is further agreed that the final payment shall be subject to the Owner’s approval of the Work. Contractor shall notify the Owner at the time of the final Construction Draw Request that the work is ready for inspection and approval. Owner shall either approve or disapprove the work within fourteen (14) days after notice from the Contractor. It shall be Owner’s responsibility to make inspection of the Project within that time period and to make any agreed upon corrections to the work within thirty (30) days after notice that the work is completed. Contractor shall be available at reasonable times to accompany Owner on an inspection of the Project. If Owner disapproves the Work, Owner shall do so in writing, specifying in detail the nature and reasons for disapproval. All objections must have a basis in non-compliance with the Contract Documents. If Contractor agrees with any point of disapproval from the Owner, Contractor shall cure or repair the item or condition specified in the written disapproval. If the parties cannot agree on the items or conditions disapproved by Owner, the dispute shall be submitted to an arbitrator, mutually approved by the parties or appointed by a court, who shall examine the Project and the notice of disapproval, take such evidence as he desires and determine what corrections, if any, should be made. The determination of the arbitrator shall be final and non-appealable.

5.5       Final Payment and Waiver of Claim. The Owner, by making final payment, waives all claims except those arising out of faulty work appearing after substantial completion of the project and not ascertainable through inspection of the work prior to final payment. By accepting final payment the Contractor waives any and all claims except those which he has previously made in writing and which remain unsettled at the time of final payment.

5.6       Caveat. Notwithstanding the foregoing, in the event Owner withholds a progress or final payment, the amount so withheld shall not be in excess of (a) the value of the defective work not corrected or cured, (b) the amount of any claim or lien filed, or (c) the amount of any payment not properly made to subcontractors for labor, materials or equipment furnished to the Project or to the Contractor for the Project; provided, however, in the event Contractor furnishes a bond in a sufficient amount to cover any of the foregoing situations, Owner shall promptly make the progress or final payment in question.

5.7       Occupancy. Owner shall not be permitted to occupy the residence until (1) final inspections are completed by the Lending Institution, if required in the loan documentation, and by the applicable municipal department or agency, and (2) Owner makes payment or release of all funds due to Contractor.

 

Article 6.1             Duties of the Contractor

6.1.1    All construction shall comply with the plans' provisions and specifications.  All systems shall be in good working order.

6.1.2    All construction shall be completed in a workman-like manner, and shall comply with all applicable national, state, and local building codes and laws.

6.1.3    All construction shall be performed by appropriately licensed individuals to perform their said work, as outlined by law.

6.1.4    Contractor shall obtain all permits necessary for the work to be completed.

6.1.5    Contractor will provide all finish materials (windows and doors, plumbing fixtures, light fixtures, appliances, tile, stone, bath accessories, finish hardware, cabinets, and flooring materials) and will utilize the contractor's discount. Any items provided by the client must be approved by the contractor 

 

Article 6.2        Duties of the Owner 

6.2.1    The owner shall provide the contractor, by the dates specified in the attached Materials form, a full Specification book of all finish items. Please see the attached Materials form. 

6.2.2    The owner shall provide interior elevations and styles for all bathrooms, kitchens, laundry, and cabinets. Failure to provide such documents will result in delays and extra costs. 

6.2.2    Any items that the owner purchases and the builder installs are the owner's full responsibility and are excluded from all warranty work. 

 

Article 7.          Change Orders and Finish Schedules

7.1.      A Change Order is any change to the original plans and/or specifications.  All change orders need to be agreed upon in writing (Text or Email), including cost, additional time considerations, approximate dates when the work will begin and be completed, a legal description of the location where the work will be done, and agreed by both parties. 

Additional time needed to complete change orders shall be taken into consideration in the project completion date.

7.2.      Any delays or changes in finish selection schedules may delay the projected completion date.

 

Article 8.               Insurance

8.1.      The Owner will keep a property Insurance Policy on the said property to protect both the owner’s and contractor’s interests until construction is completed. 

8.2.      The Owner will purchase and maintain property insurance to the full and insurable value of the project, in case of a fire, vandalism, malicious mischief or other instances that may occur.

8.3.      The Contractor will maintain needed Workman’s Compensation and Liability insurance coverage as required by law and deemed necessary for his own protection.

 

Article 9.               General Provisions

9.1.      If conditions are encountered at the construction site which are subsurface or otherwise concealed physical conditions or unknown physical conditions of an unusual nature, which differ naturally from those ordinarily found to exist and generally recognized as inherent in construction activities, the Owner will promptly investigate such conditions and, if they differ materially and cause an increase or decrease in the Contractor’s cost of, and/or time required for, the performance of any part of the work, Owner will bear the cost of any adjustment in the contract sum, contract time or both.

 

Article 10.             Hazardous Materials, Waste And Asbestos

10.1.    Both parties agree that dealing with hazardous materials, waste or asbestos requires specialized training, processes, precautions, and licenses. Therefore, unless the scope of this agreement includes the specific handling, disturbance, removal, or transportation of hazardous materials, waste, or asbestos, upon discovery of such hazardous materials the Contractor shall notify the Owner immediately and allow the Contractor to contract with a properly licensed and qualified hazardous material contractor. Any such work shall be treated as a Change Order resulting in additional costs and time considerations.

 

Article 11.             Arbitration of Disputes

11.1.    Any controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this contract, or the breach thereof, shall be settled by arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Construction Industry Arbitration Rules, and judgment on the award rendered by the arbitrator(s) may be entered in any court having jurisdiction thereof.

 

Article 12.             Warranty

12.1.    Warranty of the construction project against defects in workmanship or materials utilized shall be handled between the Owner and Subcontractors and/or Suppliers directly without involving the Contractor.  The manufacturer's warranty will prevail. No legal action of any kind relating to the project, project performance, or this contract shall be initiated by either party against the other party after (1 year) beyond the completion of the project or cessation of work. 

 

Article 13.             Termination of the Contract

13.1.    Should the Owner or Contractor fail to carry out this contract, with all of its provisions, the following options and stipulations shall apply:

13.1.1  If the Owner or the Contractor shall default on the contract, the non-defaulting party may declare the contract is in default and proceed against the defaulting party for the recovery of all damages incurred as a result of said breach of contract, including a reasonable attorney’s fee.  In the case of a defaulting Owner, the Earnest money herein mentioned shall be applied to the legally ascertained damages.

13.1.2  In the event of a default by the Owner or Contractor, the non-defaulting party may state his intention to comply with the contract and proceed for specific performance.

13.1.3  In the case of a defaulting Owner, the Contractor may accept, at his option the earnest money as shown herein as liquidated damages, should earnest money not cover the expenses to date, the Contractor may make claim to the Owner for all work executed and for proven loss with respect to equipment, materials, tools, construction equipment, and machinery, including reasonable overhead applicable to the property less the earnest money.

 

Article 14.             Attorney Fees

14.1.    In the event of any arbitration or litigation relating to the project, project performance, or this contract, the prevailing party shall be entitled to reasonable attorney fees, costs, and expenses.

 

Article 15.             Acceptance and Occupancy

15.1.    Upon completion, the project shall be inspected by the jurisdiction, Owner and the Contractor, and any repairs necessary to comply with the contract documents shall be made by the Contractor.

15.2.    The Owner shall not occupy the property until final payment has been received by the Contractor and a Certificate of Occupancy has been obtained.

15.3.    Occupancy of the project by the Owner in violation of Article 15.2, shall constitute unconditional acceptance of the project and a waiver of any defects or uncompleted work.
